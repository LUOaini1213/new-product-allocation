# Decision brief

Generated by `scripts/make_docs.py`; every number is traced to `results/*.json` by `tests/test_docs.py`. Personal project on public data; not affiliated with any company.

## English

**Question.** For a new product, how many units will sell in the first 12 weeks, and how should the first batch be split across 7 channels?

**Evidence.** 822 launches identified in public retail data; 437 of them are forecast out of time, each one only from launches whose 12 weeks had already ended before its launch month.

**Recommendations.**

- Forecast before launch with the median of the 10 most similar earlier launches (description text and price). MALE 1.147 against 1.239 for the global median and 1.235 for gradient boosting: the complex model is not worth deploying here.
- Quote the forecast as a range. The 80% interval covered 82.6% of held-out launches, and its upper bound is about 64 times its lower bound, so a single number hides most of the risk.
- Re-forecast as soon as 2 weeks of sales exist: MALE of remaining demand falls from 2.015 (no update) to 0.744 (Bass curve with the pre-launch forecast as prior); the plain curve ratio gives 0.800.
- Split the first batch by expected marginal value rather than by historical share: +0.95% value [+0.61%, +1.30%] with the same stock and the same business rules. The gain is small: one channel holds 77.3% of demand, so there is little to re-split.
- Keep 30% of the batch at the DC and release it after week 4: +5.3% value and -40.3% stock stranded in channels, at the price of a fill rate of 44.4% instead of 46.9%. If DC stock is worth no more than stranded stock the value gain is only +0.25%.

**What limits the result.** With stock set from the forecast, even a hindsight split fills 51.0% of demand: the forecast, not the split, is the bottleneck. Cost parameters are assumptions; recorded sales stand in for demand.

## 中文

**问题。** 新品上市后前 12 周能卖多少？首批库存如何在 7 个渠道之间分配？

**依据。** 在公开零售数据中识别出 822 个新品，其中 437 个按上市月份滚动留出：预测每个新品时，只用其上市月之前已走完 12 周的新品。

**建议。**

- 上市前用最相似的 10 个历史新品（品名文本+价格）的中位数做预测：MALE 1.147，全局中位数 1.239，梯度提升 1.235；复杂模型在这里不值得上线。
- 预测要给区间：80% 区间在留出新品上的覆盖率为 82.6%，上界约为下界的 64 倍，只报一个数会掩盖大部分风险。
- 有了 2 周销量就重新预测：剩余需求的 MALE 从 2.015（不更新）降到 0.744（带先验的 Bass 曲线），曲线比例法为 0.800。
- 首批分货按期望边际价值而不是按历史占比：同样库存、同样业务规则下价值 +0.95%（区间 [+0.61%, +1.30%]）；单一渠道占需求的 77.3%，可重新分配的空间小，增益不大。
- 留 30% 在仓、第 4 周后按实际销量补货：价值 +5.3%，渠道滞留库存 -40.3%，代价是满足率从 46.9% 变为 44.4%。若在仓库存并不比滞留库存更值钱，价值增益只有 +0.25%。

**限制。** 库存按预测设定时，即使事后最优的分法也只能满足 51.0% 的需求：瓶颈在预测，不在分货。成本参数为假设；销量被当作需求。
